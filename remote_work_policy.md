# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

All employees are eligible for remote work after completing their 90-day probation period. Roles that require physical presence (facilities, lab, hardware) are documented separately and are not covered by this policy.

## Modalities

The company supports three remote work modalities:

1. **Hybrid** (default): 2 days office + 3 days remote per week. The specific office days are agreed with the direct manager.
2. **Fully remote**: 100% remote work. Requires manager approval and quarterly performance review alignment.
3. **In-office**: 5 days per week in the office. By employee choice.

Employees may change modalities once per quarter with 30 days notice.

## Equipment

The company provides, for remote workers:
- Laptop (refreshed every 36 months)
- External monitor (one-time)
- Ergonomic chair reimbursement up to R$1,500 (one-time)
- Monthly internet stipend of R$150

Equipment must be returned within 10 business days of employment termination.

## Working hours and availability

Remote employees must be reachable during **core hours: 10:00 - 16:00 BRT**. Outside those hours, flexible schedules are allowed as long as weekly deliverables are met.

Status should be set accurately on Slack (available / in a meeting / focus time / away).

## Remote work during vacation

Remote work and vacation are **mutually exclusive**. An employee on vacation is on vacation — not expected and not allowed to perform work tasks. Attempting to work during paid vacation violates labor law and voids the vacation period.

For employees traveling during vacation who wish to extend their trip with additional remote work days afterwards, those days count against vacation balance or require separate approval.

## International remote work

Working from outside Brazil requires 60-day advance notice and tax/legal review. Maximum of 90 days per calendar year.
