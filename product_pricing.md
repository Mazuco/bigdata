# Product Pricing (2026)

## Plans

The company offers three paid plans plus a free tier:

### Free
- Up to 3 users per workspace
- 1 GB storage total
- Community support
- No SLA

### Starter — R$89/user/month (billed yearly) or R$109/user/month (monthly)
- Up to 25 users per workspace
- 50 GB storage per user
- Email support (48h response)
- 99% uptime SLA
- Basic integrations: Slack, Google Workspace, Jira

### Business — R$199/user/month (yearly) or R$249/user/month (monthly)
- Unlimited users
- 500 GB storage per user
- Priority support (8h response)
- 99.9% uptime SLA
- All Starter integrations + Salesforce, HubSpot, SAP
- SSO (SAML 2.0)
- Audit logs (90-day retention)

### Enterprise — Custom pricing (contact sales)
- Everything in Business
- 99.99% uptime SLA
- Dedicated customer success manager
- Audit logs (unlimited retention)
- Custom integrations
- On-premise deployment option
- Data residency (BR, US, EU)

## Trial and discounts

New customers receive a **30-day trial of the Business plan**, no credit card required.

**Volume discounts** apply automatically on the Business plan:
- 50-99 users: 10% off
- 100-249 users: 15% off
- 250+ users: 20% off

**Annual billing** gives an additional 15% discount compared to monthly billing.

## Payment and billing

Accepted payment methods:
- Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) — all plans
- PIX — monthly plans only
- Boleto — annual plans only
- Wire transfer — Enterprise only

Invoices are issued at the start of each billing cycle. Late payments incur a 2% fine plus 1% monthly interest.

## Cancellation

Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime, effective at the end of the current billing cycle. Annual plans can be cancelled with 30 days notice; refunds are prorated for the remaining full months.

Enterprise contracts follow the specific terms negotiated in the MSA.

## Add-ons

- Extra storage: R$30 per 100 GB per month
- Advanced analytics: R$49/user/month (Business and Enterprise only)
- API-only access: R$2,500/month flat fee for 1M requests/month
