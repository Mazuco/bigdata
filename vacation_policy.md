# Vacation Policy

## Entitlement

All full-time employees are entitled to **30 calendar days of paid vacation per year**, available after completing 12 months of continuous service.

Employees with less than 12 months accrue 2.5 days per month and may use accrued days with prior approval from their manager.

## Scheduling

Vacation requests must be submitted at least **15 business days in advance** through the HR portal. Requests during peak business periods (December 15 – January 15, July 1-31) require 30 days notice.

Managers may deny requests only in writing and only for documented business reasons.

## Splitting vacation

Employees may split their 30 days into up to **three separate periods**. At least one period must be a continuous block of **10 consecutive days** to comply with Brazilian labor law (CLT Art. 134).

## Payment

Vacation pay includes:
- Regular salary for the vacation period
- A one-third (1/3) constitutional bonus (added to the base salary)
- Payment issued up to 2 business days before the vacation starts

## Carry-over

Unused vacation days do not carry over past the 24-month limit. Days not taken within 24 months after accrual will be paid as a mandatory cash-out, with a 100% penalty surcharge (double pay) as required by law.

## Vacation during probation

Employees in the first 90 days of employment cannot schedule vacation except in exceptional circumstances approved by HR.
